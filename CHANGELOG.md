# Changelog

## 1.0.0 · 22 October 2026

- Explore far from home, solo or with up to seven friends.
- Choose travel distance, gear storage and optional travel-home rules.
- Pause and return home, then resume with your saved expedition gear and XP.
- Manage invitations, group settings and return through `/farbound`.
- English menus with player heads, tooltips, opacity settings and vanilla sounds.
